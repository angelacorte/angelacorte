About me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering of the University of Bologna, under the supervision of Danilo Pianini and the co-supervision of Mirko Viroli.

My research focuses on advances in collective robotics through macro-programming. I am interested in aggregate computing, self-organising systems, distributed control, and the tools required to move from high-level coordination models to practical robotic systems.

I am also a contributor to Collektive, a Kotlin Multiplatform implementation of aggregate computing based on the exchange calculus.

I am a member of the Pervasive Software Lab (PsLab), where we work on intelligent, adaptive, and scalable distributed systems.
